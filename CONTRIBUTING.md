# Contributing

`clin_longsum` is developed and maintained solely by Eukairos.

This repository does not accept pull requests. 

We welcome

- **Bug reports and reproductions.** Issues describing what broke, with the stage, the artifact,
  and the subject/hadm involved. 
- **Questions about pipeline behaviour**, particularly where `References/PIPELINE.md` is wrong or
  unclear.
- **Security reports**

Any contribution accepted by exception will be taken only under a **written copyright assignment
to Eukairos** (or its successor entity), executed before the work is merged. The terms are:

1. The contributor assigns all copyright in the contribution to Eukairos worldwide.
2. The contributor warrants the work is original, is theirs to assign, and is not encumbered by
   an employer, institution, or prior license.
3. Eukairos may license the contribution under any terms, including terms other than the AGPL.
4. The contributor is credited in the release notes unless they ask not to be.

Contributions made without a signed assignment will not be merged. Nothing in this file grants any licence to Eukairos's copyright, and nothing here is an offer to contract.

## Data

Never attach clinical data, cohort CSVs, ledgers, compaction output, or any excerpt of a patient
record to an issue or any other public channel. Reproduce defects with `subject_id` / `hadm_id` references and stage
names only, never with note text. See `LICENSING.md`.
